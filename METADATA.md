Description 
This study investigates the impact of various treatments including antibiotics and, 
dietary metabolites on the gut microbiome composition in a mouse model.

Mice were grouped according to treatment type, timepoint, and experimental condition, and were maintained using controlled diets. Sample collection occurred at the pre-treatment phase to establish baseline microbial profiles. Key treatments included antibiotics (Metronidazole, Vancomycin, Neomycin), microbial metabolites (Butyrate, Succinate), DMF, and a disease-inducing agent (AOM_DSS), with untreated mice serving as controls.
